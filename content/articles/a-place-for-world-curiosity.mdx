The world is full of details that become more interesting when we pause long
enough to notice them: a street plan shaped by centuries of trade, a museum
object that changes how a familiar period looks, or a technology that quietly
alters daily life.

## Looking closely

World Curiosity is a place to collect those observations. Travel may provide a
starting point, but the purpose is to follow a subject wherever it leads:
through history, culture, art, food, technology, and ideas.

This sample article exists to establish the writing workflow. It demonstrates
the metadata every future article needs while keeping the article body simple
and readable.
